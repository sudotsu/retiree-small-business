## Overview

Three funnels:

1. **Cold → Week 1** — for new subscribers (quiz completers, free plan downloaders, landing page signups) who have not yet started the course
2. **Week 1 → Mastery** — for students who completed or are in Week 1 (Essentials or Module 1) but have not enrolled in Mastery
3. **Post-purchase welcome** — for new Mastery and Essentials enrollees (sets expectations, reduces early dropout)

All three are plain-language, peer-to-peer in tone. No urgency theater. No countdown timers. No "this offer expires" language unless a genuine deadline exists.

### Email 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** One thing that separates people who finish from people who don't

---

The people who complete this course have one thing in common: they do the exercises on paper, in the workbook, with real numbers and real answers.

Not because it is a requirement. Because it is the only way the work produces the outputs it is designed to produce.

The decision-influences drill in Lesson 1.1 takes about forty-five minutes when you do it fully. It takes ten minutes if you do it in your head without writing anything down — and produces almost nothing useful.

The lesson will keep asking you to be honest. That instruction is not rhetorical. The exercises are calibrated to give you the most accurate possible picture of your defaults, your situation, and your business — but only if you put real answers in, not aspirational ones.

You paid for this. Get the real version of it.

— Chas

## Notes for Integration

- All emails should be plain text or very light HTML — this audience does not benefit from heavy design and trusts plain more than polished
- Subject lines should be tested with and without personalization tokens (first name) — some segments respond well to first-name subjects, others find it jarring
- All links should go to specific pages (lesson 1.1, Mastery enrollment, community) — not the homepage
- Unsubscribe must be prominent and one-click — no guilt, no "are you sure?" intermediate step
- Reply address should route to a monitored inbox — Chas's note that he reads replies should be true or the email should not say it
