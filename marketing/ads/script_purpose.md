# Ad Script — Purpose
# Hook: H6 "Purpose + Profit" | Target: adults 60-75 seeking meaning after retirement

**Format:** 30-second video ad (social/pre-roll)
**Tone:** Peer-to-peer, honest, no cheerleading
**Word count:** ~90 words spoken

---

## Script

You spent thirty years getting good at something.

And now it sits there — the expertise, the judgment, the relationships — and no one is asking for it anymore.

That is a waste.

The Retiree Business Blueprint is a structured program that helps you turn what you already know into a real business — one that fits your life and pays you for the work you are uniquely qualified to do.

Week 1 is free. No payment information. No catch.

If it is right for you, you will know by the end of the week.

**RetireebusinessBlueprint.com**

---

## Alternate headline variants (A/B test)

- "Find Your Purpose Again — And Get Paid For It"
- "The Thing You're Best At Shouldn't Retire With You"
- "Your Experience Doesn't Have an Expiration Date"

---

## Production notes

- **Visual:** Real person 60–75, not stock. Quiet confidence. Not inspirational-poster energy — matter-of-fact.
- **Delivery of final line:** "If it is right for you, you will know by the end of the week." — conversational, not a pitch. True statement, said plainly.
- **Hook segment (H6):** Aligns with Purpose + Profit positioning — meaning comes first, income follows
- **Segment:** `quiz1_build_confidence`, `quiz2_service_star`, `quiz2_consulting_champion`
