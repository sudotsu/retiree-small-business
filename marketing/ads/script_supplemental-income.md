# Ad Script — Supplemental Income
# Hook: H1 "Second Act, First Clients" + H11 "Cash First, Fancy Later" | Target: retirement income realists

**Format:** 30-second video ad (social/pre-roll)
**Tone:** Direct, practical, no-nonsense
**Word count:** ~90 words spoken

---

If you want a real additional income stream — not passive income mythology, but actual work that pays — the Retiree Business Blueprint shows you how to build one around what you already know how to do.

Start there. See if it is what you need.

## Production notes

- **Visual:** Person at a desk or kitchen table, working — not lying on a beach. Income is about work, not escape.
- **Tone:** This is the pragmatic angle — treat the audience as adults who understand money and are looking for a real solution, not a lifestyle pitch
- **No income promises:** The script deliberately says "actual work that pays" — not a specific income claim
- **Hook segment (H1, H11):** Aligns with outcome-focused and cash-realism hooks
- **Segment:** `quiz3_ready_to_launch`, `quiz3_cautious_capable`
