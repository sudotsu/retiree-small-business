# Ad Script — Doubt to Doer
# Hook: H25 "From Doubt to Doer" + H3 "Low-Risk Launch" | Target: people stuck at the threshold

**Format:** 30-second video ad (social/pre-roll)
**Tone:** Direct, warm, credible — speaking to someone who already knows what they want to do
**Word count:** ~90 words spoken

---

## Script

If you have been thinking about starting a business for more than six months and have not started —

it is probably not because you do not have a good idea.

It is because you do not have a clear first step that does not feel like a bet you cannot afford to lose.

The Retiree Business Blueprint is built around low-risk, validated starts. We begin with who you are, not what the market wants.

Week 1 is free. No payment information. No commitment.

Try it before you decide.

**RetireebusinessBlueprint.com**

---

## Alternate headline variants (A/B test)

- "The First Step Shouldn't Feel Like Betting the Farm"
- "Still Thinking About It? Here Is Why."

---

## Production notes

- **Visual:** A person pausing — mid-thought, not paralyzed. The energy is contemplation moving toward decision, not anxiety
- **Key line:** "It is probably not because you do not have a good idea." — this should feel like being seen; it names the real obstacle without condescension
- **Risk language:** The script uses "validated starts" and "who you are, not what the market wants" — this connects directly to Module 1 which is the first thing a student does
- **Final CTA is soft:** "Try it before you decide" — not "sign up now." This ad is for the undecided; the CTA should honor that
- **Hook segment (H25, H3):** Doubt-to-doer + low-risk launch
- **Segment:** `quiz3_cautious_capable`, `quiz3_preparing_to_begin`, `quiz1_build_confidence`
