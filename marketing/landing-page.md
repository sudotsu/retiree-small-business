## COMMON QUESTIONS

**Is this right for someone who already has a business?**
Yes — particularly the marketing, money, and customer modules. Many students come in with something already running and use the course to build the structure they skipped when they started.

**Do I need to be tech-savvy?**
The course lives on a straightforward web platform. Each lesson is text-based with exercises you can complete on paper or digitally. If you can read this page, you can complete the course. A Tech Concierge session is available twice weekly for anyone who encounters a technical issue.

**What if I'm not sure what kind of business I want?**
Week 1 is specifically designed to help you figure that out. The default settings work and the fit evaluation are the most useful tools available for making that decision — more useful than trying to decide before you start.

**What is the time commitment?**
Essentials: approximately 10 hours across 8 days. Mastery: approximately 56 hours across 8 weeks, at roughly 7 hours per week. Both are designed to be completed in the time estimates given — not stretched across months.
