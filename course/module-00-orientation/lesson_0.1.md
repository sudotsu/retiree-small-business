# Lesson 0.1 — Welcome and Course Orientation

### Estimated Time: Approximately 60–70 minutes

*(Reading: 25 minutes | Exercises: 25 minutes | Journal: 15 minutes)*

---

## Lesson Overview

Welcome to the Retiree Business Blueprint, where your next chapter begins with clarity — not overwhelm.

This first lesson orients you to the program, helps you choose your best path (Essentials or Mastery), sets your schedule, introduces the support systems available to you, and gets you connected with your peers.

By the end of Lesson 0.1, you will have:

- A dedicated workspace and study schedule.
- A clear understanding of what the course covers and how it is structured.
- Your Tech Concierge and support channels set up.
- Your first "hour-a-day" block booked on your calendar.

---

## Part 1 — Understanding Your Two Paths

The Blueprint gives you flexibility. Both tracks lead to the same destination — a working small business — at different paces.

| Track | Description | Pace | Best For |
|-------|-------------|------|----------|
| **Essentials (7 Days)** | Crash course covering the fundamentals | Approximately 10 hours total | If you want quick progress or a refresher |
| **Mastery (8 Weeks)** | Comprehensive system from idea to launch to first profit | Approximately 1 hour per day | If you are ready to commit to a real micro-business |

If you are unsure which track to take, starting with Essentials and then continuing into Mastery is a natural path — it builds momentum and gives you a preview of the full course.

---

## Exercise 1 — Choose Your Track and Block Your Hour

**1.** Look at your calendar and find five one-hour blocks per week where you can study and reflect without interruption.

**2.** Write them into your planner or set phone reminders.

**3.** Decide: Essentials or Mastery first?

**4.** Write that decision on the first page of your workbook.

*Example: "I am choosing the Mastery track and reserving 9–10 a.m., Monday through Friday as my Business Hour."*

---

## Part 2 — How This Course Works

Each module follows the same rhythm:

**Learn** through short readings (20–30 minutes per day). The content is direct and practical — no filler.

**Reflect** through journal prompts that connect the concept to your specific life and situation.

**Apply** through exercises that produce a real output — not just completed worksheets, but usable pieces of a working business plan.

**Share** in the community to stay motivated and accountable. Your peers are going through the same material at the same time.

You will receive printable PDFs, large-print handouts, and optional video walkthroughs. Every week builds on the last.

---

## Part 3 — Your Support System

**Tech Concierge**

Real people, not bots. They help with setup — logging in, downloading PDFs, printing, or connecting to video calls. You can request a 15-minute one-on-one call or join a live lab session during Weeks 0–3.

**Office Hours**

Every Tuesday and Thursday, 2–3 p.m. Central. Drop in with questions or share your progress.

**Community**

The private course community group is where your peers share wins, ask questions, and give each other perspective. Weekly threads include Goal-Setting Monday, Win Wednesday, and Feedback Friday. Respond with encouragement and respect the privacy of others.

**Contact**

For anything urgent: email support@retireebusinessblueprint.com or call (402) 812-3294. Response within one business day.

## Exercise 2 — Set Up Your Workspace

**Goal:** Create a physical space that tells your brain "this is my business time."

**1.** Pick a consistent spot — a kitchen table, a desk, a corner of a room.

**2.** Remove clutter. Add something that is motivating to look at — a photograph, a plant, something that represents what you are working toward.

**3.** Keep your supplies nearby: pen, notebook, printed worksheets.

**4.** Optional: set up a small jar or container near your workspace. Each lesson, drop in a folded note with one small win. These accumulate. Looking back at them in Week 4 or Week 8 is worth doing.

Your environment signals to your brain what to expect. A consistent space builds focus faster than willpower does.

## Journal Prompts

Take fifteen minutes to write full responses to these questions. Full sentences, not bullet points.

**1.** What made you decide to take this course now — at this point in your life?

**2.** What does success look like for you 90 days from now? Not in business terms — in daily life terms.

**3.** Where can you realistically protect an hour each day without resentment or guilt?

**4.** Who in your life is most likely to encourage you when motivation slips? Is there anything you need to ask of them before this course begins?
