# Module 1 — Knowing Yourself: Your Foundation and Decision-Making

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 1**

---

## Welcome to Week 1

This week is not about business ideas, marketing plans, or technology. It is about you — specifically, about understanding how you are wired well enough to build something that fits your life rather than works against it.

Most businesses that stall do so because the person running them has not accounted for their own patterns: how they respond to risk, what actually motivates them, whose opinions they give weight to, and what they believe about money and worth. This module addresses all of that before anything else — not as theory, but as a working foundation you will use throughout the rest of the course.

By the end of Week 1, you will have:

- A written map of the decision drivers and default settings that have shaped your choices for decades
- An honest evaluation of which of those defaults help and which create friction
- A set of personal operating rules — guardrails — to carry through Modules 2 through 8
- A clear, honest readiness decision about your pace going into Module 2
