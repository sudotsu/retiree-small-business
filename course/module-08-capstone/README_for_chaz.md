# README for Chaz — Module 8: Putting It All Together

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 8**

---

**lesson_8.1.md** — Managing for Success. Students assess the three areas that determine whether a business sustains itself — people (every significant relationship in and around the business), things (all equipment, systems, and operational elements), and money (budgeting, cash flow, and regular financial review). A self-rating table and honest gap analysis are the core of the exercise. Complete.

**lesson_8.2.md** — Knowing When You Need Help. Five questions that surface where the owner is spending time on work that someone else could do, and where outside help (outsourced or hired) would free capacity for the core work. Students produce a prioritized list of help needs and a clear answer to whether they need help now. Complete.

**lesson_8.3.md** — Your Long-Term Business Strengths. Three questions address genuine long-term strengths (with external evidence, not just self-assessment), what will sustain the student in the business physically, emotionally, and relationally over years, and how to set a pace that is sustainable given their real-life circumstances. Complete.

**lesson_8.4.md** — Your Exit Strategy. First-draft exit strategy using the Where/Who/What framework: how location-dependent the business is, whether the business value is transferable to another owner, and whether the product or service will remain viable over the long arc. The lesson argues that building a business worth selling is the same as building one worth running. Complete.

**lesson_8.5.md** — Your Business Action Plan. The capstone of the entire course. Students review all eight modules, identify the most important output from each, and write formal behavioral objectives (personal, profits, promotional) with what, how, and by when. The lesson ends with a commitment to where the plan will be kept and a date to revisit it. **One item needed from you before publishing:** your personal story replacing the prominent placeholder block in the reading section (between Part 2 and Exercise 1). 150–200 words, on the value of a written, visible, revisited action plan. See the instructor guide for the full theme notes.
