# Module 8 — Putting It All Together

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 8**

---

## What This Module Is About

Seven weeks of work has built something real: a foundation of self-knowledge, a business concept, a time strategy, a marketing plan, a financial baseline, a lead generation system, and a customer retention strategy. Module 8 asks what you do with all of it.

The answer is not another analysis. It is a plan — a specific set of commitments, drawn from everything you have built, that you will actually use.

This final module covers the three management areas that determine whether a business sustains itself (people, things, money), the question of where you need outside help, the long-term strengths that will carry the business over years, the first draft of an exit strategy, and the capstone exercise: a complete, behavioral action plan drawn from all eight weeks of work.

---

## Before You Start

Bring everything. This module — especially Lesson 8.5 — asks you to review and draw from all eight previous modules. Have your workbooks or notes from Modules 1 through 7 accessible before you begin Lesson 8.5.

## What You Will Produce This Week

By the time you complete Module 8, you will have:

- A three-area management assessment with honest ratings and a specific improvement commitment
- A prioritized list of areas where outside help would benefit your business most
- A written articulation of your genuine long-term strengths and your sustainable pace
- A first-draft exit strategy covering the Where, Who, and What factors
- A complete behavioral action plan — at least three objectives (personal, profits, promotional) with what, how, and by when — drawn from eight weeks of work

The action plan is the final deliverable of the course. Print it, post it somewhere visible, and set a date to revisit it.
