Per-file details for everything in this folder. For a quick overview and file table, see `READ-THIS-FIRST.md`.

## lesson_4.1.md – lesson_4.5.md

The five daily lessons for Week 4. Each is a complete, self-contained lesson: reading content, exercises, a named story, journal prompts, a community prompt, a 5-question quiz, a checklist, and a transition to the next lesson. These go into LearnDash as individual pages, unlocking in order.

The sequential dependency matters more here than in most modules — Lesson 4.5 (Promotion) explicitly uses outputs from Lessons 4.1 through 4.4 (benefits from the RAW evaluation, working price, delivery step, position sentence). Students who skip ahead have no material to draw from.

Time estimates: 4.1–4.4 (~75–80 min each), 4.5 (~80–85 min — synthesis lesson with two exercises). Correct quiz answers are marked *(Correct)* in the source — that label gets removed on entry into the quiz builder.
