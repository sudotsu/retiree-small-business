# Module 2 — Painting the Big Picture

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 2**

---

## What This Module Is About

In Module 1, you looked inward. You mapped the decision drivers, default settings, and internal patterns that have guided your choices throughout your life.

In Module 2, you look outward.

This week you will build two pictures: one for your life as a whole, and one for your business specifically. You will learn to see them clearly, describe them honestly, and identify the changes most worth pursuing. By Friday, those changes will be written into five specific, dated objectives that will guide your business activity in the weeks ahead.

This is the week where intention becomes direction.

---

## What You Will Be Able to Do by the End of This Module

- Identify the people, activities, and roles that currently make up your life — including those that support you and those that drain you.
- Describe your life and your business in sensory terms precise enough to reveal the gap between where you are and where you want to be.
- Understand why your business belongs inside your life's big picture — not the other way around — and build it that way from the start.
- Write five behavioral objectives using a three-part formula that makes them specific, accountable, and impossible to misinterpret.
- Assign realistic due dates to each objective based on their importance and how ready you are to begin.

## A Note Before You Begin

One idea runs through every lesson this week: your business is a part of your life. It is not a replacement for it.

This matters more than it might sound. Many people who build a business after retirement — even people who are thoughtful and well-prepared — gradually allow the business to displace the very things that motivated them to start it: the freedom, the relationships, the sense of purpose and contribution. They end up re-creating the most draining aspects of the career they left, without intending to.

The work you do this week is designed to prevent that. You will build your business picture explicitly within the context of the life you want to live. Every objective you write will be anchored to that picture.

That structure is not limiting. It is protective. A business built to fit your life will last. One built at the expense of it rarely does.
