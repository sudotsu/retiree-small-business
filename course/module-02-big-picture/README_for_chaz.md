# Module 2 — File Reference

Per-file details for everything in this folder. For a quick overview and file table, see `READ-THIS-FIRST.md`.

---

## lesson_2.1.md – lesson_2.5.md

The five daily lessons for Week 2. Each is a complete, self-contained lesson: reading content, exercises, a named story, journal prompts, a community prompt, a 5-question quiz, a checklist, and a transition to the next lesson. These go into LearnDash as individual pages, unlocking in order. Correct quiz answers are marked *(Correct)* in the source — that label gets removed on entry into the quiz builder.

Time estimates vary by lesson: 2.1 (~75 min), 2.2 (~70–75 min), 2.3 (~75–80 min), 2.4 (~85–90 min), 2.5 (~80–85 min). Lessons 2.4 and 2.5 are the most demanding of the week.

---

## Module_2_Student_Facing.md

The module landing page — what students see before opening any lesson. Covers what the week is about, what they need, time estimates, and how to get help. Becomes the Module 2 section page on the course website, with workbook download links and a "Start Lesson 2.1" button.

---

## module_2_workbook.md

All five lessons' exercises compiled into one print-ready document — worksheets only, no lesson prose. Needs PDF export before students can download it. Standard specs: 16pt minimum, 1.5 line spacing, Open Sans or similar, 0.75-inch margins, letter-size. Also a large-print version at 18pt. Both go in the Resources section of the landing page.

---

## module_2_instructor_guide.md

Facilitation playbook — not published to students. Covers what each lesson is trying to accomplish, common student struggles, community patterns to watch for, office hours topics, and video recording notes. Includes placement guidance for the personal story in Lesson 2.3 (your own experience mapping your life and business big picture together — the highest-value personal moment of the week).

---

## module_2_learndash_plugin.md

Technical setup brief for your web person. Covers module structure, lesson prerequisite chain, quiz configuration, workbook PDF settings, landing page setup, accessibility checklist, and community thread titles with facilitation prompts.

## module_2_status.md

Production checklist. Items marked [x] are complete; [ ] are pending. Platform and accessibility items (LearnDash entry, PDF upload, testing) are your web person's side. Instructor items (videos, personal story) are yours.
