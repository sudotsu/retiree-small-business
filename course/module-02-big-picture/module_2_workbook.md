## How to Use This Workbook

Work through each exercise in lesson order — 2.1, then 2.2, and so on. Do not skip ahead. Each exercise builds on what came before it, and Lessons 2.4 and 2.5 require your completed work from Lessons 2.1 and 2.3.

You do not need to print this entire workbook at once. If you prefer, print one lesson's exercises at a time.

Bring this workbook to each lesson session. The reading in the online lessons will guide you through every exercise — this document gives you the dedicated writing space to complete them.
