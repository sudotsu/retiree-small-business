# Module 2 — Your Big Picture

Week 2. Students build a clear picture of their life and business — where they are now, where they want to be — and write five specific objectives to bridge the gap. These five objectives are the foundation every module from here builds on.

## Personal Story This Week

**Lesson 2.3** — your own experience mapping your life's big picture and your business together: what you saw, what surprised you. The instructor guide covers timing and placement. This is the most memorable moment of the week for most students.

## Video Worth Considering

Lessons 2.3, 2.4, and 2.5 are the three highest-value video targets this week — 2.4 and 2.5 are the two longest lessons (85–90 min each) and where students most often need a human voice alongside the written content.
