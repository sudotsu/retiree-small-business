# Module 6 — Finding Customers

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 6**

---

## What This Module Is About

Every business has to find the people who will buy from it. That does not happen automatically, and it does not happen the same way for every business.

Module 6 gives you a system for generating leads — not by telling you which channel is universally best, but by walking you through the process of matching the right channels to your specific customers. By the end of this week, you will have evaluated every major lead generation option available to a small business, built a detailed profile of your typical customer, sharpened your core message for that customer, and produced a written one-year plan with real costs attached.

The goal is not activity. It is a deliberate strategy you will actually follow.

---

## Before You Start

Bring your Module 4 materials to this module. Specifically, you will want access to the core product message you built in Module 4 — Lesson 6.3 will ask you to revise it for your profiled customer.

If you have existing customers, think about your five most recent ones before you begin Lesson 6.2. Their characteristics will be the raw material for your customer profile.

## What You Will Produce This Week

By the time you complete Module 6, you will have produced:

- A completed evaluation of twelve lead generation channels against your specific business and budget
- A written Typical Customer Profile based on nine specific questions about your customers
- A revised version of your core product message, tailored to the customer you profiled
- A media match analysis filtering all twelve channels against your customer profile
- A one-year lead generation calendar with your top channels, activation months, costs, and a first-thirty-days commitment

These are working documents, not exercises. The profile and the plan belong in your business files and should be revisited when your customer base changes significantly.
