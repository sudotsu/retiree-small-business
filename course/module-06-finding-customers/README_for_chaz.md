# README for Chaz — Module 6: Finding Customers

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 6**

---

**lesson_6.1.md** — The Lead Generation Toolkit. Students evaluate twelve lead generation channels (word of mouth, newspapers, magazines, press releases, telemarketing, direct mail, trade shows, internet, radio, television, billboards, telephone directories) against their specific business and budget, then identify their top three candidates with reasoning and activation notes. Complete.

**lesson_6.2.md** — Building Your Customer Profile. Students answer nine specific questions to build a written Typical Customer Profile describing who their typical customer is — age, income, geography, media habits, interests, and more. The lesson ends by checking whether their five most recent customers fit the profile. Complete.

**lesson_6.3.md** — The Message to Your Profiled Customer. Students take the core product message from Module 4 and rewrite it specifically for the customer they profiled in 6.2 — using that customer's vocabulary, addressing their situation, and passing an "objection test" that checks whether the message handles the customer's most likely hesitation. Complete.

**lesson_6.4.md** — Matching Media to Your Customer. Students re-evaluate the twelve channels from 6.1 using the profile from 6.2 as a filter, distinguishing between channels that genuinely reach their customer and those that reflect personal preference. A second dimension sorts channels into Day One (produces results quickly) and Builds Over Time. **One item needed from you before publishing:** your personal story from your advertising and media background, replacing the `[KORB STORY HERE]` placeholder in the reading section. 150–200 words. The instructor guide describes the theme. Everything else is complete.

**lesson_6.5.md** — Your One-Year Lead Generation Strategy. The capstone of Module 6. Students select their top three primary channels, build a twelve-month activation calendar with cost estimates, and commit to a specific first-thirty-days action with a date. Complete.

**Module_6_Student_Facing.md** — The module landing page students see before starting Lesson 6.1. Lists all five lessons with time estimates, what students need to bring (Module 4 message, knowledge of recent customers), what they will produce, and support contact. Complete — update the contact placeholder before publishing.

**module_6_workbook.md** — All exercises from all five lessons compiled into a single printable document. Needs to be exported to PDF and attached to the Module 6 landing page as a download. Complete.

**module_6_instructor_guide.md** — Your facilitation reference. Covers what each lesson does, common student patterns for each lesson, what to watch for, and the reminder about inserting your story in Lesson 6.4. Also notes the Module 4/5/7 connections. Not for students.
