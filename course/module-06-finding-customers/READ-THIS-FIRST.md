# READ THIS FIRST — Module 6: Finding Customers

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 6**

Module 6 is the customer acquisition week. Students evaluate twelve lead generation channels, build a customer profile, tailor their message for that customer, match media to the profile, and produce a written one-year strategy with costs.
