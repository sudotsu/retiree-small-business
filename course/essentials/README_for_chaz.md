# README for Chaz — Essentials Track

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Essentials Track**

---

**day_1.md** — Day 1: Your Business and Your Default Settings. Students assess the four categories of internal wiring — attitudes, beliefs, motivations, and key people — and decide whether their defaults currently support a business. The exercise produces an honest written assessment, not a scoring rubric. Complete.

**day_2.md** — Day 2: Painting the Big Picture. Students map six categories (People, Activities, Roles, Flavors, Colors, Sounds) in two columns — as things are now versus as they want them to be. The gap between the two columns is the main product of the exercise. Complete.

**day_3.md** — Day 3: Putting Time in Checkmate. Students rewrite their weekly schedule as a template — not a day-by-day appointment book, but a master plan showing where business time, family time, and personal commitments live in a typical week. Includes identification of time-wasters and non-negotiables. Complete.

**day_4.md** — Day 4: Marketing and Promotion. Students work through the five Ps (Product, Pricing, Propagation, Positioning, Promotion) to build their marketing message foundation, then plan where and how they will promote it. Includes an optional draft advertisement exercise. Complete.

**day_5.md** — Day 5: Cash and Profits. Two-part exercise. Part one: actual cash flow from real bank statements, three months. Part two: a working budget with revenues, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and net. Students answer the essential question about what adjustments could improve profitability. The longest day — 90 to 105 minutes. Have bank statements ready. Complete.

**day_6.md** — Day 6: You Need Leads to Succeed. Students assess twelve lead generation channels against two questions (would I use it? can I afford it now?) and commit to one specific action this week. Includes an extended section on word of mouth / referral strategy. Complete.

**day_7.md** — Day 7: Keeping Customers. Students build a five-part customer retention strategy: how to convert leads, what service looks like, how to think about lifetime value, how to turn best customers into advocates, and how to stay on customers' radar between purchases. Complete.

**day_8.md** — Day 8: Putting It All Together. The capstone. Students write three behavioral objectives (personal, profits, promotional) with what/how/by when, then complete the Wrap Up: priorities, contacts, key changes, discoveries, and where the document will be kept and reviewed. The closing section includes a written bridge to the Mastery Track — soft language, no hard sell. Adjust the tone of that closing if you want it stronger or softer. Complete.

**essentials_student_facing.md** — The course landing page. Lists all eight days with time estimates, what students need to prepare (bank statements, a printer, quiet time), and what they will produce. One placeholder to update before publishing: replace `[INSTRUCTOR CONTACT]` with your preferred contact information.

**essentials_workbook.md** — All eight exercises compiled for print. Students should download and print this before Day 1. Export to PDF and attach to the course landing page.

**essentials_instructor_guide.md** — Facilitation notes for all eight days: what to watch for, how to handle resistance, where the exercises tend to produce the most useful results, and the connection from each day to the corresponding Mastery module. Not for students.

**essentials_learndash_plugin.md** — Configuration reference for the web person. Includes all lesson settings, quiz configuration, checklist settings, course completion trigger, and specific guidance for setting up the Essentials as a free course if that is the access model you choose. Not for students.

**essentials_status.md** — Production checklist. Two items needed before publishing: update the instructor contact placeholder, and export the workbook to PDF. All eight lessons are otherwise complete.
