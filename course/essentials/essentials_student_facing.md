# Retiree Business Blueprint — Essentials Track

**Eight days. Your complete business foundation.**

---

## What Is This Course?

The Essentials Track is a one-week intensive for retirees who want a clear, honest picture of their business — fast.

In eight days you will work through every major area of a small business: who you are as an owner, what your life looks like, how your time is organized, what your marketing message is, where the money stands, how you find customers, how you keep them, and how to put all of it into a written plan you will actually use.

This is not a survey course. Each day produces something real — a written output you keep, not a quiz you pass. By the time you finish Day 8, you will have a full business snapshot and a set of specific commitments for what happens next.

Total time: approximately eight to ten hours, spread across eight days (roughly one hour per day, with Day 5 running longer).

---

## What You Will Need

Before you begin, have the following ready:

- Bank statements and receipts from the last two to three months (needed for Day 5)
- Something to write with — exercises are designed for printing and handwriting
- A reasonably quiet place to work each day
- Your full attention for each session — these exercises reward honesty, not speed

---

## What You Will Produce

By the end of this course you will have:

- An honest assessment of whether your default settings support a business right now
- A clear picture of your business and life as they are and as you want them to be
- A rewritten weekly schedule that protects business time, family time, and personal time
- A written marketing foundation: product, pricing, delivery, positioning, and promotional plan
- A three-month cash flow assessment and a working budget
- A lead generation strategy with one committed action
- A customer retention plan covering all five stages of the customer relationship
- Three written business objectives — personal, profits, and promotional — with what, how, and by when

---

## A Note Before You Begin

The exercises in this course are only useful if you are honest. The point is not to produce a document that looks like a business plan. The point is to know where you actually stand and make real decisions based on that.

The business owners who get the most from a course like this are the ones who write the uncomfortable answer, not the polished one.
