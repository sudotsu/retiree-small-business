# Module 3 — Putting Time in Checkmate

Week 3. Students audit their actual weekly schedule, identify and cut what's wasting their time, and build a revised schedule designed around what matters most. They leave Friday with a working template they can post on their wall.

The most personally honest week of the course. The facilitation matters more here than any other week.
