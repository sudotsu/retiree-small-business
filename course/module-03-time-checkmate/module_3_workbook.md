**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track**

*This workbook contains every exercise from Lessons 3.1 through 3.5. Use it alongside the online lessons, or print it and work from paper. All exercises are formatted with writing space included.*

*Large-print version available for download from the Resources section of your course page.*

*Recommended print settings: Letter size, portrait orientation, margins at least 0.75 inches on all sides. Lessons 3.1 and 3.5 contain full-page schedule grids — print those pages in landscape orientation for more writing space if preferred.*

## How to Use This Workbook

Work through each exercise in lesson order. Do not skip ahead — the exercises build on each other in sequence, and Lessons 3.2 through 3.5 depend directly on the schedule you complete in Lesson 3.1.

Bring your work from Modules 1 and 2 to every session this week. You will need your default settings (Lessons 1.2 and 1.4) for Lesson 3.2, and your five objectives (Lesson 2.5) for Lessons 3.4 and 3.5.
