## lesson_3.1.md – lesson_3.5.md

The five daily lessons for Week 3. Each is a complete, self-contained lesson: reading content, exercises, a named story, journal prompts, a community prompt, a 5-question quiz, a checklist, and a transition to the next lesson. These go into LearnDash as individual pages.

Module 3 uses a stricter prerequisite chain than other modules — each lesson individually requires the previous one, not just module-level access. The reason: every lesson builds directly on the prior one's output, and students who skip ahead have nothing to build Lesson 3.5 from. The LearnDash config file covers the setup.

Lessons 3.1 and 3.5 both contain full weekly schedule grids and run 85–90 minutes — longer than the others. A brief time warning at the top of each page helps prevent mid-exercise abandonment. Lessons 3.2, 3.3, and 3.4 run 75–80, 75–80, and 75 minutes respectively.

Correct quiz answers are marked *(Correct)* in the source — that label gets removed on entry into the quiz builder.

## Module_3_Student_Facing.md

The module landing page — what students see before opening any lesson. Includes time estimates for all five lessons, a note that students need their Modules 1 and 2 workbooks this week, and workbook download links. Becomes the Module 3 section page with a "Start Lesson 3.1" button.

## module_3_workbook.md

All five lessons' exercises compiled into one print-ready document — includes both full weekly schedule grids (Lessons 3.1 and 3.5). Needs PDF export. The two schedule grid pages print significantly better in landscape orientation; if the PDF tool supports mixed-orientation pages, those two pages go landscape and the rest stay portrait. If not, two separate PDFs work — one landscape for the grids, one portrait for the rest. Standard specs: 16pt minimum, 1.5 line spacing, 0.75-inch margins. Also a large-print version at 18pt.

## module_3_instructor_guide.md

Facilitation playbook — not published to students. Covers what each lesson is trying to accomplish, common student struggles (including the two most likely sources of community difficulty: schedule avoidance in 3.1 and uncomfortable naming in 3.3), office hours topics, and video recording notes. Includes placement guidance for two personal story moments: the corporate friend story (Lesson 3.2) and the time-waster story (Lesson 3.3 — the `<KORB STORY HERE>` from your original source material, the most emotionally resonant personal moment of the week).
