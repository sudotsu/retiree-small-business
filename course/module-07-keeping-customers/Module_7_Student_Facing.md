# Module 7 — Keeping Customers

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 7**

---

## What This Module Is About

Finding customers is the front end of a business. Keeping them is the back end — and for most small businesses, the back end is where durable revenue is built.

Module 7 covers the complete arc of the customer relationship after the lead is generated: how to close the gap between a lead and a paying customer, how to serve customers in a way that makes them return, how to think about what each customer is actually worth over the full course of the relationship, how to develop your most enthusiastic customers into active advocates, and how to stay visible to your customers between transactions so they do not drift to someone else.

---

## Before You Start

Review your Module 6 materials before beginning. You will reference your customer profile (Lesson 6.2) and your profiled message (Lesson 6.3) in Lesson 7.1 when developing your conversion pitch. If you have not completed Module 6, do that first.

Think about your current customers before you begin Lesson 7.4. The evangelist development exercise works best with real customers in mind — have five names you can write down when you get there.

## What You Will Produce This Week

By the time you complete Module 7, you will have produced:

- A written conversion pitch, built from six specific elements, that you can use immediately with real leads
- A customer service strategy covering all five components of how customers experience your business
- A lifetime value calculation for your typical retained customer
- A written development plan for five specific potential evangelists in your customer base
- A complete five-part Customer Retention Strategy with a specific this-week action commitment

These are working documents. The retention strategy belongs in your business files and should be revisited at least annually.
