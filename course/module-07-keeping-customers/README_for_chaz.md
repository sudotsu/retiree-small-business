# README for Chaz — Module 7: Keeping Customers

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 7**

---

**lesson_7.1.md** — Turning Leads into Customers. Students learn the six elements that convert a lead into a paying customer — engaging in comfort zones, addressing head and heart, describing the specific need, framing benefits rather than features, pre-empting the most likely objection, and making a clear offer. Two exercises: a pitch development worksheet and a full written pitch narrative. Complete.

**lesson_7.2.md** — Customer Service That Keeps Customers. Students build a customer service strategy across five components (people, phone, web, communications, and other), assessing both what they are already doing and what they need to add. Uses the Jeff story — a high school employee in an Iowa clothing store whose genuine effort and respect produced lifelong customer loyalty — to illustrate how personal service creates return business. Complete.

**lesson_7.3.md** — The Lifetime Value of a Customer. Students calculate the full lifetime value of a typical retained customer (transaction value × frequency × years), then outline the three practices that extend customer relationships: communicating ongoing value, providing clear ways to connect, and making customers feel genuinely valued. Second exercise: a draft letter to their top customers. Complete.

**lesson_7.4.md** — Turning Your Best Customers into Evangelists. Students identify five of their most enthusiastic customers and write a specific plan for each, covering three elements: detailed product information to share, a gesture of privileged relationship, and a recognition plan for their referrals. The lesson emphasizes that recognition — not payment — is what activates most potential evangelists. Complete.

**lesson_7.5.md** — Staying on Your Customers' Radar. The synthesis lesson. Students write a five-part Customer Retention Strategy consolidating all of the module's work, then commit to a single specific action they will take before the week ends. Complete.
