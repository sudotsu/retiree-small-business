# Module 5 — The Art of Making Money: Budgets, Cash Flow, and the Strategy for Profit

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 5**

## Welcome to Week 5

Andy Warhol once said: "Making money is art, and good business is the best art."

He was not talking about greed. He was talking about the discipline of running something well — of treating the financial side of a business with the same seriousness and skill as any other craft.

This week is that discipline. Not complicated accounting. Not a pile of spreadsheets. A clear, honest picture of where your money comes from, where it goes, when the gaps appear, and what to do about them.

By the end of Week 5, you will have:

- A three-month baseline of your revenues and expenses, organized and categorized
- A working list of the seasonal, demographic, and behavioral trends already present in your business
- A forward set of probable changes layered into your planning at the specific months they are expected
- A cash flow assessment showing the actual movement of real money — and the timing gaps between what you earn and what you receive
- A completed three-month working budget with a net profit row and at least two specific adjustments that improve your margin
