# README — Module 5: The Art of Making Money

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track**

---

**lesson_5.1.md through lesson_5.5.md** are the five student-facing lesson pages for Week 5. The arc: build a three-month baseline of revenues and expenses (5.1) → identify the trends already present in the business (5.2) → make probable-change predictions and layer them into the forward view (5.3) → assess actual cash movement and find the timing gaps (5.4) → combine everything into a working three-month budget with a specific profit strategy (5.5). Time estimates are content-based and vary: 5.1 and 5.5 are 85–90 min (large financial grids), 5.4 is 80–85 min, 5.2 and 5.3 are 75–80 min.

**module_5_workbook.md** compiles all exercises from all five lessons into a single printable document, including all three-month financial worksheet grids. This is the file that exports to PDF for students. Because students write actual financial numbers into this workbook, the PDF should be high quality and the grid cells large enough to write in. A fillable PDF version is an option worth considering for students who prefer to keep records digitally.

**Module_5_Student_Facing.md** is the landing page students see when they begin Week 5. It includes a note about what to bring to Lesson 5.1 (three months of bank statements and receipts) and a data privacy reassurance. The support contact fields are placeholders that need current values before publishing.

**module_5_instructor_guide.md** covers facilitation notes for all five lessons, the most common student patterns Chaz will see (students who estimate instead of checking actuals, students who cannot distinguish fixed from variable, students who show net losses and panic), and a personal story placement note for Lesson 5.5. One Chaz story is needed before this module publishes — the guide describes where it goes and what theme fits best.

**module_5_learndash_plugin.md** is the full LearnDash configuration reference. Notable items: the lesson preparation notes that should display before Lessons 5.1, 5.4, and 5.5 (students need their financial records before starting those sessions), and the data privacy statement for the module landing page.

**module_5_status.md** is the production checklist. The main pending item before publishing is the Chaz personal story for lesson_5.5.md.

**README_for_chaz.md** is this file. **READ-THIS-FIRST.md** is the quick-reference navigation table for the folder.
