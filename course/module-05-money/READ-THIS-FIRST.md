# READ THIS FIRST — Module 5: The Art of Making Money

**Retiree Business Blueprint | Mastery Track | Week 5**

Module 5 is the financial clarity week. Students build a three-month revenue and expense baseline, identify trends and probable changes, assess cash flow timing, and complete a working budget with a profit strategy.
