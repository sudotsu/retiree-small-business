# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 8
# Putting It All Together

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Seven days.

You have worked through every major area of your business.

[pause 1s]

Most people who start a business

never do any of this systematically.

You have done it in a week.

[pause 2s]

Now you do the one thing

that determines whether any of it becomes real.

[pause 1s]

You write it down as commitments.

[pause 2s]

Walter finished a course like this one

and put the workbook on the shelf.

[pause 1s]

He came back to it eight months later

when the business had drifted

in directions he did not understand.

[pause 1s]

Everything that needed to happen

was in the workbook, in his own handwriting.

He just hadn't kept it anywhere he could see it.

[pause 1s]

In year three, he printed his objectives

and taped them to the inside of his office door.

[pause 1s]

"The plan didn't get better.

I just finally put it somewhere I couldn't ignore it."

[pause 2s]

Three objectives today:

personal, profits, and promotional.

[pause 1s]

Each one with what, how, and by when.

[pause 1s]

Then the Wrap Up —

your three immediate priorities,

the most important discovery you made this week,

and where this document will live.

[pause 1s]

Not where it will be safe.

Where it will be visible.

[pause 2s]

And if you want to go deeper on any area this week —

the Mastery Track is built exactly for that.

[pause 1s]

But first: open Day 8.

Write the objectives.

Decide where the document lives.

Make the commitment.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:15–2:30 at relaxed pace
- **Delivery:** Walter's quote ("I just finally put it somewhere I couldn't ignore it") is the throughline for the whole capstone day — deliver it with quiet emphasis; the Mastery mention should feel like an open door, not a sales pitch; the final three-line instruction is the close — land it with finality
