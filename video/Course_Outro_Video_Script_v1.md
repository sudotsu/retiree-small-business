# Course Outro Video Script (v1)

**Purpose:** Closing video — appears after Lesson 8.5 completion
**Instructor:** Chas (on camera)
**Target length:** ~2:30–3:00 minutes at 125–130 WPM
**Word count:** ~370 words

---

Eight weeks.

Forty lessons.

A foundation that most business owners spend years assembling — if they assemble it at all.

You know your decision-making defaults and the guardrails that keep your business from fighting your grain. You know what you want the picture of your life to look like, and you have five objectives pointed at closing the gap. You have a schedule that protects what matters. You have worked through every dimension of marketing for your specific product and your specific customers. You have looked honestly at your finances and built a working budget with a strategy for profit. You have a lead generation plan with real costs and a first commitment. And you know what your customers are actually worth over the full course of a relationship — not one transaction at a time.

That is not a small thing. That is a complete business foundation.

---

What you do with it now is up to you.

I cannot tell you the business will work. Nobody can tell you that. What I can tell you is that the decisions you make from here — about your product, your pricing, your time, your customers — will be better decisions because you have done this work. Not because the course made you a better person. Because you now have information you did not have before. And good decisions run on good information.

Keep the action plan from Lesson 8.5 somewhere visible. Revisit it in ninety days. When you look at it, ask one question: did I do what I said I would do? If yes, write the next set. If no, write it again — more honestly this time.

---

This was built to give you a fair start. I hope it did that.

Now go build something good.

---

### Production Notes

- **Camera:** locked mid-shot, eye level, soft warm light — same setup as module intros
- **Delivery:** this is not a ceremonial graduation speech — keep the register level and genuine; "good decisions run on good information" is the intellectual anchor; "Now go build something good" is the final line and should land as a sincere send-off, not a motivational slogan; no music swell, no fade-to-black dramatics — just the close
