# Teleprompter Script — Module 1 Intro
# Knowing Yourself: Your Foundation and Decision-Making

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Think back to a significant decision

you made in the last ten years.

[pause 1s]

Not a small one.

A career change. A move.

Something you had to really sit with.

[pause 1s]

---

You didn't flip a coin.

[pause 2s]

You considered who it would affect.

What it would cost.

How it sat against your values.

[pause 1s]

Maybe what certain people would think —

a few of whose opinions

carry more weight than you'd choose.

[pause 2s]

You ran it through a process.

A system you've been using

your whole adult life

to make decisions that matter.

[pause 2s]

---

**That system is what this week maps.**

[pause 1s]

Not to fix it —

it's gotten you here.

[pause 1s]

But a business that fits your life

has to fit the person making the decisions.

[pause 2s]

Most people have used that system for so long

they've never stopped to describe

how it actually works.

[pause 1s]

This week, you describe it.

[pause 2s]

---

Here's the honest reason this comes first.

[pause 1s]

The most common reason

small businesses stall —

not fail, but stall —

isn't a bad idea.

[pause 1s]

It's misfit.

[pause 2s]

The person running the business

has built something

that works against their own grain.

[pause 1s]

I've run my own businesses long enough

to have seen this happen —

in myself, and in others.

[pause 1s]

The people who struggle longest

are almost always fighting themselves somewhere.

[pause 2s]

This week builds the map

so you can build _with_ your grain,

not against it.

[pause 2s]

---

Five lessons.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 1.1** — you map your decision influences

and narrow to the three that matter most.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 1.2** — you organize them

into four categories:

attitudes, beliefs, motivations, and people.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 1.3** — the most demanding session of the week.

Every default has a positive side

and a negative one.

You'll look at both, honestly,

across all four categories.

[pause 1s]

Plan 85 to 90 minutes for it.

One sitting if you can.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 1.4** — you rewrite

the settings that create the most friction,

and build personal guardrails

to carry into every module ahead.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 1.5** — your decision gate.

An honest readiness check

before you move to Module 2.

[pause 2s]

---

One thing before you start.

[pause 1s]

This week works best

when you write what's actually true —

not what sounds right,

not what looks better on paper.

[pause 1s]

You've been running this system for decades.

You know how it actually performs.

[pause 1s]

The goal is to see it clearly enough

to use it on purpose.

[pause 2s]

---

Print the Module 1 workbook

before you open Lesson 1.1.

All five lessons draw from it.

[pause 1s]

Then open Lesson 1.1.

Read the overview.

Start the drill.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** 3:30–4:00 at relaxed pace (~110–115 words/min spoken)
- **Camera:** locked mid-shot, eye level, soft warm light
- **Delivery:** unhurried, direct — this module sets a reflective tone, not an energizing one; resist the urge to pick up pace
- **Slide cues (optional):**
  - [SLIDE 1] "Module 1 — Knowing Yourself"
  - [SLIDE 2] "The Misfit Problem"
  - [SLIDE 3] "Five Lessons This Week"
  - [SLIDE 4] "Honesty Over Speed"
