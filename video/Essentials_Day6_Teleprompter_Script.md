# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 6
# You Need Leads to Succeed

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

More good businesses fail from insufficient lead generation

than from bad products or poor service.

[pause 1s]

The business works.

There just aren't enough people finding out about it.

[pause 2s]

Rita spent her first two months

building a website, designing a logo,

getting her LinkedIn profile in shape.

[pause 1s]

At the end of month two she had one client —

a friend who felt obligated.

[pause 1s]

She had been preparing to be found.

She had not been generating leads.

[pause 2s]

A former colleague suggested something different.

Rita identified twenty-five people in her network

who worked in industries where transitions were common.

[pause 1s]

She sent each of them a personal note —

not a mass email —

explaining what she was doing

and asking if they knew anyone who might benefit.

[pause 1s]

Within three weeks she had seven clients.

[pause 1s]

Nothing changed except

she stopped waiting and started asking.

[pause 2s]

Today's exercise lists twelve channels.

For each one: would I use this, and can I afford it now?

[pause 1s]

Most will not pass both filters. That is fine.

You need a short list, not a long one.

[pause 1s]

After the channels —

one specific action you will take this week.

[pause 1s]

Not "work on word of mouth."

The action. Named specifically.

By when.

[pause 2s]

Open Day 6.

Work through the twelve channels honestly.

Then commit to one action.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:00–2:15 at relaxed pace
- **Delivery:** Rita's story should move quickly to its point; "she stopped waiting and started asking" is the pivot line; the "one action, named specifically, by when" close is instruction, not inspiration — deliver it that way
