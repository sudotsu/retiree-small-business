# Teleprompter Script — Module 5 Intro
# The Art of Making Money

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Andy Warhol once said

that making money is art,

and good business is the best art.

[pause 1s]

He was not talking about greed.

[pause 1s]

He was talking about discipline —

treating the financial side of a business

with the same seriousness and skill

as any other craft.

[pause 2s]

Most people do not bring that attention

to the money side of their business.

[pause 1s]

Not because they don't care —

but because the financial numbers feel like something

that happen to a business

rather than something you actively manage.

[pause 1s]

This week, you stop letting them happen to you.

[pause 2s]

---

One distinction this week will ask you to hold clearly

that most small business owners blur

until it causes real problems.

[pause 1s]

A business can be profitable on paper

and simultaneously out of cash.

[pause 2s]

Profit is a calculated concept —

sometimes counting income that has not yet been collected.

[pause 1s]

Cash is a physical fact:

money that has landed in your account

and is available to spend.

[pause 1s]

When a client owes you money but has not paid —

that is profit.

It is not cash.

[pause 2s]

The lesson on cash flow this week

begins with a story from Forbes.

[pause 1s]

A young financial controller presenting the books,

showing a solid profit.

[pause 1s]

The elder Mr. Forbes looked at the report and said:

[pause 1s]

"I don't give a damn what your books show.

Do we have any money in the bank?"

[pause 2s]

That question is the subject of Lesson 5.4.

[pause 2s]

---

Before the lessons:

bring your bank statements from the last three months

before you open Lesson 5.1.

[pause 1s]

You don't need them organized.

The lesson walks through that.

[pause 1s]

But without the actual numbers,

the lesson cannot be completed honestly.

[pause 2s]

---

Five lessons.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 5.1** — revenues, expenses, and your first budget picture.

[pause 1s]

Three months of actual numbers, organized and categorized.

[pause 1s]

Plan 85 to 90 minutes.

This lesson contains a three-month grid

that requires your real data, not estimates.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 5.2** — spotting trends.

[pause 1s]

The seasonal shifts and behavioral patterns

already present in your business.

[pause 1s]

You know some of this intuitively.

This lesson asks you to name the pattern.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 5.3** — predicting outcomes.

[pause 1s]

Probable changes

layered in at the specific months you expect them.

[pause 1s]

That's what separates planning from guessing.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 5.4** — cash flow.

[pause 1s]

The real vital sign.

[pause 1s]

Susan, in this week's mini-story,

had twelve clients, strong referrals, and clear profitability.

[pause 1s]

In September of her second year,

she could not pay herself.

[pause 1s]

None of the causes were business problems.

All three were timing problems —

completely predictable once she mapped them.

[pause 1s]

This lesson shows you how to map them.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 5.5** — the build.

[pause 1s]

A three-month working budget.

A net profit row.

[pause 1s]

And the question this whole week has been building toward:

[pause 1s]

given an honest picture of your money,

what is your strategy

for making this business produce a sustainable profit?

[pause 2s]

---

By the end of this week

you will have a complete, honest picture

of your business's financial health —

revenues, expenses, cash timing, trends, and the way forward.

[pause 1s]

Bring your bank statements.

Then open 5.1.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** 3:30–4:00 at relaxed pace (~110–115 words/min spoken)
- **Camera:** locked mid-shot, eye level, soft warm light
- **Delivery:** the Forbes quote is the anchor — deliver it as a direct quote, not a summary; pause before "Do we have any money in the bank?" and let it fully land before continuing; the rest of the script is measured and precise; this is a craft module, not a motivational one — the register should feel like a seasoned operator, not a coach
- **Slide cues (optional):**
  - [SLIDE 1] "Module 5 — The Art of Making Money"
  - [SLIDE 2] "Making money is art, and good business is the best art — Andy Warhol"
  - [SLIDE 3] "Profitable on paper. Out of cash."
  - [SLIDE 4] "Cash flow: the real vital sign"
  - [SLIDE 5] "Your strategy for sustainable profit"
