# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 3
# Putting Time in Checkmate

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

You have a picture of where you want to go.

Now you have to deal with the one thing

that determines whether you ever get there.

[pause 1s]

Time.

[pause 2s]

Retirement removes the scheduling backbone

that work used to provide.

[pause 1s]

Without it, time expands to fill whatever space it's given.

And a business without protected, predictable hours

runs on whatever is left over after everything else —

which is usually not much.

[pause 2s]

Frank started a tax preparation service at seventy-one.

Two seasons of chaos.

Clients at all hours.

Files on the kitchen table.

[pause 1s]

The problem wasn't volume.

He had never established

when the business existed and when it did not.

[pause 1s]

In year three, he built a template:

Monday through Thursday, 9 AM to 1 PM,

January through April.

No files after 1 PM.

[pause 1s]

Best revenue.

Calmest household.

Four afternoons a week free.

[pause 1s]

"The schedule didn't limit the business.

It made the business possible to keep."

[pause 2s]

What you build today is not a day-by-day planner.

It is the architecture of a typical week.

[pause 1s]

Non-negotiable commitments go on first.

Time-wasters get named honestly.

Business hours get assigned as real blocks —

not aspirational ones.

[pause 1s]

Open Day 3.

Plan the week you will actually keep.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:00–2:15 at relaxed pace
- **Delivery:** Frank's condensed story should be clipped — quick, clear facts building to the payoff quote; the final instruction ("plan the week you will actually keep") should land with gentle firmness
