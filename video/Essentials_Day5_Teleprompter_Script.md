# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 5
# Cash and Profits

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Before you open this lesson:

pull out your bank statements and receipts

from the last two or three months.

[pause 1s]

Have them on the table before you start.

[pause 2s]

Today you face the numbers.

Not projections. Actual numbers.

[pause 2s]

There is one distinction this day will ask you to hold clearly.

[pause 1s]

Profit is what the books show.

Cash is what is actually in your account.

[pause 1s]

A business can be profitable on paper

and simultaneously unable to pay its bills.

[pause 1s]

That is not a theoretical problem.

It is one of the most common reasons

healthy-looking small businesses close.

[pause 2s]

Gordon's woodworking shop was doing well by every visible measure.

More orders than he could fill.

Award from a regional crafts association.

[pause 1s]

He had no cash flow tracking.

[pause 1s]

In October of year three,

revenues dropped to near zero for four weeks.

Fixed expenses kept running.

He came very close to shutting down

a business that was genuinely good.

[pause 1s]

Once he started tracking,

he discovered a predictable seasonal pattern

he had never seen because he had never looked.

[pause 1s]

The business is the same.

The information is different.

[pause 2s]

Day 5 has two parts:

cash flow, then budget.

[pause 1s]

This is the longest day of the course.

Set aside uninterrupted time.

[pause 1s]

The numbers are information.

Not a verdict.

[pause 1s]

Open Day 5.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:00–2:15 at relaxed pace
- **Delivery:** "The business is the same. The information is different." is the close of the Gordon story and a key line — let it land; the bank statements instruction at the top is practical and should be said plainly, not rushed
