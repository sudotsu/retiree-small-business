# Teleprompter Script — Module 2 Intro
# Your Big Picture

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

The question most people want to ask right now is:

_what should my business be?_

[pause 1s]

That question is coming.

[pause 1s]

But to answer it in a way that holds up —

a year from now, two years from now —

you need to see the full picture first.

[pause 1s]

Not the business picture.

The life picture.

[pause 2s]

That's what this week builds.

[pause 2s]

---

Last week you looked inward.

Decision drivers, defaults, guardrails —

the invisible mechanics of how you're wired.

[pause 1s]

This week you look outward.

[pause 1s]

Two pictures:

one for your life as it currently exists,

and one for your business placed inside it.

[pause 1s]

By Friday, the gap between those two pictures

will be converted into five specific, dated objectives.

[pause 1s]

Not intentions. Not a vision.

Five commitments — with names and deadlines.

[pause 2s]

---

Before the lessons, one principle worth stating directly.

[pause 1s]

**Your business belongs inside your life.**

**Not the other way around.**

[pause 2s]

That sounds obvious.

It is harder in practice than it sounds.

[pause 1s]

I've watched capable, motivated people —

people who started well and planned carefully —

allow their business to gradually displace

the very things that motivated them to build it.

[pause 1s]

It doesn't happen in one decision.

It happens through a series of reasonable trade-offs.

[pause 1s]

Over a year or two,

those trade-offs accumulate into a life

that looks a great deal like the career they left —

minus the salary.

[pause 2s]

That's a failure of structure, not character.

And it's entirely preventable

if you build the structure correctly at the beginning.

[pause 1s]

This week builds it correctly.

[pause 2s]

---

Five lessons.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 2.1** — a full inventory of your life as it is:

every significant person in it,

how you actually spend your time,

and the roles through which others know you.

Plan the full time. The list goes longer than people expect.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 2.2** — the one that surprises people.

You'll describe your life in sensory terms:

flavors, colors, sounds.

[pause 1s]

There's a difference between knowing your schedule is overwhelming

and being able to say it tastes like burnt coffee —

functional, but consumed out of habit more than enjoyment.

[pause 1s]

The second description creates urgency to change it.

The first just produces a to-do item.

[pause 1s]

The gap between where your life is

and where you want it to be

is your real motivation for the week.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 2.3** — the same framework applied to your business.

The people, the activities, the roles — present and desired.

[pause 1s]

The essential question underneath all of it:

does this business picture fit inside the life picture,

or does it start to crowd out what belongs there?

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 2.4** — picture-building ends, action begins.

Five behavioral objectives

using a three-part formula:

specific target, specific behavior, specific person responsible.

[pause 1s]

"I want to grow my client base"

is not an objective.

[pause 1s]

"I will add eight new clients in four months

by personally following up with every referral within 24 hours"

is an objective.

[pause 1s]

That's the difference this lesson teaches.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 2.5** — due dates.

[pause 1s]

A trip to see family stays in "soon"

until there's a date on the calendar.

The moment that date gets booked,

arrangements get made and the trip becomes real.

[pause 1s]

Objectives work the same way.

This lesson assigns the right dates —

realistic, differentiated, honest about when you're actually ready.

[pause 2s]

---

By the end of this week:

a clear picture of your life,

a clear picture of your business inside it,

and five objectives with names and deadlines.

[pause 1s]

The picture work is finished after this module.

Everything that follows builds on top of it.

[pause 2s]

---

Print the Module 2 workbook

before you open Lesson 2.1.

More writing space gets used this week than last.

[pause 1s]

Then open 2.1.

Start the inventory.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_
