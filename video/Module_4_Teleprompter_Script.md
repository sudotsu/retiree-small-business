# Teleprompter Script — Module 4 Intro
# Marketing: Five P's in a Pod

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

There is a reason the marketing section of every small business course gets the most attention.

[pause 1s]

It is also the reason most people do it wrong.

[pause 2s]

Marketing has a reputation for complexity it doesn't deserve.

[pause 1s]

Decades of consultants making marketing seem complicated

have contributed to that reputation —

because complexity is profitable for the people selling the solution.

[pause 1s]

For you, it is just noise.

[pause 2s]

---

Three weeks of internal work —

who you are, what you want, a schedule to support it.

[pause 1s]

This week the course turns outward.

To the people you are building this for.

[pause 2s]

---

One principle that runs through every lesson this week.

[pause 1s]

Marketing fails most often

not because the message is wrong —

but because the order is wrong.

[pause 1s]

People skip to promotion —

the ad, the post, the pitch —

before they have done the thinking promotion depends on.

[pause 1s]

Promotion amplifies whatever is underneath it.

[pause 1s]

Make sure what is underneath it holds up.

[pause 2s]

---

Five lessons.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 4.1** — your product through your customer's eyes.

[pause 1s]

Your product is your baby.

You care about it genuinely, and that care is real.

[pause 1s]

But your customer doesn't weigh your product

against how long you worked on it.

[pause 1s]

They weigh it against everything else available to them

that solves the same problem.

[pause 1s]

The framework is called RAW —

Relevance, Accessibility, and Worth.

[pause 1s]

You will evaluate your product on all three

from your customer's perspective,

then from your own,

then name the gap between the two.

[pause 1s]

That gap is the map of the work ahead.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 4.2** — pricing.

[pause 1s]

A three-step process grounded in what the customer actually values,

what it costs you,

and what the market around you looks like.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 4.3** — propagation.

[pause 1s]

The complete path your product takes from your business to your customer.

Every step. Every friction point.

[pause 1s]

One of this week's stories — Dorothy —

had customers who loved her product

and simply couldn't figure out how to get more.

[pause 1s]

The product was not the problem.

The path was.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 4.4** — positioning.

[pause 1s]

The place your business currently occupies in your customer's mind —

and the place you want to earn.

[pause 1s]

Your Module 1 default settings are part of this exercise.

The position that lasts is the one built on who you actually are.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 4.5** — promotion.

[pause 1s]

Your message, in four components.

And a first draft advertisement —

real language, real product, real ask.

[pause 1s]

That exercise generates more community discussion

than anything else this week.

[pause 1s]

Post the draft.

Not the polished version.

[pause 2s]

---

By the end of this week

you will have a complete marketing foundation —

all five P's worked through for your specific business.

[pause 1s]

Download the workbook.

Then open 4.1.

Start with your product through your customer's eyes.

[pause 1s]

Be honest about the gap.

That is where all useful marketing work begins.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** 3:30–4:00 at relaxed pace (~110–115 words/min spoken)
- **Camera:** locked mid-shot, eye level, soft warm light
- **Delivery:** register shift — after three reflective internal modules, this is where the course turns outward; slightly more crisp than M1–M3 without being hard-edged; "Promotion amplifies whatever is underneath it" is the thesis line — let it land before moving; "Be honest about the gap" closes with weight, not punishment
- **Slide cues (optional):**
  - [SLIDE 1] "Module 4 — Marketing: Five P's in a Pod"
  - [SLIDE 2] "Promotion amplifies whatever is underneath it"
  - [SLIDE 3] "RAW — Relevance · Accessibility · Worth"
  - [SLIDE 4] "The Five P's: Product · Price · Propagation · Position · Promotion"
  - [SLIDE 5] "Post the draft. Not the polished version."
