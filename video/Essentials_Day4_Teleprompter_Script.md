# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 4
# Marketing and Promotion

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Today the course turns outward.

[pause 1s]

What your business is,

what it costs,

how it reaches customers,

and what makes it worth choosing.

[pause 2s]

Vivian had catered for two years

without knowing why people hired her.

[pause 1s]

A friend asked: "Who is your customer, exactly?"

[pause 1s]

Vivian said: "Anyone who needs catering."

[pause 1s]

Her friend said: "That is not a customer.

That is a population.

Who, specifically?"

[pause 1s]

What emerged was this:

families planning milestone celebrations

for people over sixty.

[pause 1s]

She had been doing this for two years

without knowing it.

[pause 1s]

That was her positioning.

It had been true the whole time.

She just hadn't said it.

[pause 2s]

Five P's:

[pause 1s]

Product — what you sell, in two sentences a stranger understands.

Price — what you charge, and why that signals the right value.

Propagation — how the product gets to the customer.

Positioning — what makes you the obvious answer for your specific customer.

Promotion — how the public learns about you.

[pause 1s]

Promotion comes last.

Deliberately.

[pause 1s]

Promoting before the other four are clear

is one of the most expensive mistakes a small business makes.

[pause 2s]

Open Day 4.

Work through each section specifically.

Vague answers produce vague results.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:00–2:15 at relaxed pace
- **Delivery:** Vivian's exchange is conversational and should feel like a real dialogue; "She just hadn't said it" lands as a quiet revelation; the five P's list can move quickly — the audience just heard the framework, not the detail
