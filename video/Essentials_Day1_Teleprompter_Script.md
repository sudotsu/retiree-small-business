# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 1
# Your Business and Your Default Settings

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Before you build a business,

there is one question worth asking

that almost nobody asks.

[pause 1s]

Are you actually wired for this?

[pause 2s]

Everyone has default settings —

internal wiring that shapes how you respond

before you've had time to think.

[pause 1s]

Not permanently fixed.

But operating in the background,

whether you examine them or not.

[pause 1s]

Today you examine them.

[pause 2s]

Four categories:

[pause 1s]

Attitudes — how you respond under pressure.

Beliefs — what you hold to be true

about what someone like you is capable of.

Motivations — what is actually driving you,

not the answer that sounds good when someone asks.

And People — the specific individuals

whose opinions factor into your decisions.

[pause 1s]

Some of them will support a business.

Some will not.

[pause 1s]

Know which is which before you start.

[pause 2s]

Nora had everything she needed

to run a consulting practice.

The only thing holding her back

was a belief she had never examined.

[pause 1s]

Once she saw it clearly,

she found out it did not hold up.

[pause 1s]

She has been consulting for four years.

[pause 2s]

The question is not a verdict.

It is information.

You can work with information.

[pause 1s]

Open Day 1.

Work through all four categories honestly.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:00–2:15 at relaxed pace (~110–115 words/min spoken)
- **Delivery:** warm but direct — Day 1 sets the tone for the whole course; the question "Are you actually wired for this?" should land as genuine, not intimidating; Nora's conclusion should feel quietly triumphant
