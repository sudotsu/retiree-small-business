# Teleprompter Script — Module 3 Intro
# Putting Time in Checkmate

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Think about the last week you described as busy.

[pause 1s]

If someone asked you to account for

where the time actually went —

hour by hour, honestly —

how accurate would that account be?

[pause 2s]

For most people: not very.

[pause 1s]

The gap between where we think

our time is going

and where it's actually going

is almost always larger than we expect.

[pause 2s]

This week you find out exactly how large it is —

and you do something about it.

[pause 2s]

---

Two weeks ago you mapped your internal wiring.

Last week you built your life and business pictures

and wrote five objectives to close the gap.

[pause 1s]

Both of those weeks produced something real.

This week is where that work gets a home.

[pause 2s]

---

Here's the principle that runs through everything this week.

[pause 1s]

Life fills available time

the way water fills a container.

[pause 1s]

Whatever space is not deliberately claimed

gets claimed by something else —

not by bad intent,

but by the natural pressure

of everything already in your life.

[pause 2s]

If the things you identified as most important

over the last two weeks

are not specifically in your schedule,

they will not happen.

[pause 1s]

The only way to change that

is to design the schedule

instead of inheriting it.

[pause 2s]

---

One thing worth saying before the lessons.

[pause 1s]

This is not time management in the generic sense.

[pause 1s]

You are not building a schedule

to optimize efficiency in the abstract.

[pause 1s]

You are building a schedule

designed around the specific defaults

and big picture goals

you've already worked to understand.

[pause 1s]

A generic productivity template

doesn't know about your relationship with creativity,

or your commitment to the people who matter most.

[pause 1s]

Your schedule after this week will.

[pause 2s]

---

Five lessons.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 3.1** — the honest audit.

Your full current week, written out:

sleep, meals, family, health, habits, everything.

[pause 1s]

Plan 85 to 90 minutes.

Two schedule grids.

Don't start unless you have the full time.

The accuracy of everything that follows

depends on how honest you are in this one.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 3.2** — the six anchors.

Three from your Module 1 default settings.

Three from your Module 2 big picture goals.

[pause 1s]

These are the fixed points

your revised schedule gets built around.

[pause 1s]

I've watched people build schedules

that systematically suppressed

the things that kept them functioning well —

and the cost shows up in ways

that take years to undo.

The anchors prevent that.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 3.3** — the most uncomfortable exercise of the week.

[pause 1s]

You will name at least seven things

in your current schedule

that are consuming time without earning it.

[pause 1s]

It doesn't require ruthlessness.

It only requires asking honestly, for each item:

am I choosing this, or just continuing it?

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 3.4** — two commitments, both non-negotiable.

Time for your business,

and time for yourself.

[pause 1s]

Most people assume business time

is the harder one to protect.

In practice it's almost always the other way around.

[pause 1s]

Business time has urgency behind it.

Personal time can always happen next week.

[pause 1s]

The cost of deferring it

doesn't show up immediately.

But it compounds.

[pause 1s]

**Lesson 3.5** — the build.

Your revised schedule —

a master template,

not a day-planner.

The floor plan of your week,

not the furniture arrangement.

[pause 1s]

One specific thing going in:

do not revise the template

the first time it conflicts with real life.

Give it four weeks.

That's what separates a schedule that lasts

from one that gets abandoned

the first time a Tuesday goes sideways.

[pause 2s]

---

By the end of this week,

your objectives from Module 2 have a home.

Your defaults and big picture goals

have protected time.

[pause 1s]

And the schedule you carry

into everything that follows

is one you built on purpose.

[pause 2s]

---

Print the Module 3 workbook

before you open Lesson 3.1.

Two full schedule grids are in there.

[pause 1s]

Then open 3.1.

Have 90 minutes.

Write what is actually true.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_
