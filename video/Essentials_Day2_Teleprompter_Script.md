# Teleprompter Script — Essentials Day 2
# Painting the Big Picture

_(Camera on. Warm lighting. Chas seated, relaxed, direct eye contact.)_

---

Yesterday you looked inward.

Today you look outward.

[pause 1s]

Everything in the frame —

your life as it actually is right now,

and the way you want it to look.

[pause 2s]

Earl retired, started a handyman service,

and by month three was working fifty-hour weeks.

His wife was eating dinner alone.

He hadn't been to a single grandchild's Saturday game.

[pause 1s]

He built exactly what he said he didn't want.

[pause 1s]

The problem was not that he was too busy.

It was that he had never defined

what "enough" looked like.

[pause 1s]

He had a business but no picture.

So he filled every available hour,

because there was nothing telling him when to stop.

[pause 2s]

Today's exercise gives you the picture

before that drift starts.

[pause 1s]

Six categories:

people, activities, roles —

and three that work differently:

flavors, colors, and sounds.

[pause 1s]

Don't skip the last three.

They access how you feel about your situation

in ways direct description sometimes misses.

[pause 1s]

For each category:

as it is, and as I want it to be.

[pause 1s]

The gap between those two columns

is what today is really producing.

[pause 2s]

Be honest about the current picture.

That is the only way the gap is accurate.

[pause 1s]

Open Day 2.

[pause 2s]

_(fade out)_

---

### Production Notes

- **Run time:** ~2:00–2:15 at relaxed pace
- **Delivery:** Earl's story should carry emotional weight without melodrama; the pivot ("he had a business but no picture") is the key insight; the close is clear and actionable
