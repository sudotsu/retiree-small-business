## What to Review

**Course content — recommended approach**
You do not need to read every lesson. The fastest path:

1. Open any module folder (e.g., `rbb/module-01-foundation/`)
2. Read `READ-THIS-FIRST.md` — a one-page table showing every file, who it's for, and what it contains
3. Read the `Module_X_Student_Facing.md` — the student landing page for that module. This tells you what the week promises and in what order.
4. Spot-check one or two lessons if you want to verify tone or accuracy

The content was written to treat your students as capable adults with decades of experience. If that register is off anywhere, flag it.

**Video scripts — read these carefully**
These are what you will say on camera. Read both versions for any module before you record:
- Full narrative: the complete script at conversational pace (~7 minutes per module intro, ~4:30 per Essentials day)
- Teleprompter: shorter lines and pause markers, designed to read aloud naturally

Each script references a mini-story from the corresponding lesson. The stories are named characters (fictional composites) — you can use them as written, riff on them, or swap in your own version. The teleprompter notes where to do this.

**Marketing — review for accuracy and voice**
- `marketing/site/landing.md` — the main landing page. Check that the track descriptions, pricing structure, and your bio placeholder reflect what you want.
- `marketing/ads/` — three 30-second video ad scripts. Check that the positioning matches how you want to introduce the course.
- `marketing/email/funnels.md` — the three email funnels. Verify the tone sounds like you. The emails sign off as "Chas" and include a line that you read replies — that should only stay if it's true.
